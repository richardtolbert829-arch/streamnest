FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Our team is happy to help.

Contact Us
Do I have to pay to activate a streming app?

No. Activating an app on the provider's official website is free. You only need an active account or subscription with that service. Be wary of anyone who asks for payment to activate.

Where do I enter the code shown on my TV?

The app on your TV shows the exact web address to visit. Open it on your phone or computer, make sure it's the provider's official website, and enter the code there.

My code expired before I could use it. What now?

Codes usually last only a few minutes. Go back in the app on your TV and choose Sign in again to get a fresh code.

I entered the code but nothing happened on my TV.

Give it 30 seconds, then close and reopen the app. If it still doesn't work, check your internet connection, update the app and restart your device.

Are you the official support team for my TV or app?

No. We're an independent help website. For billing or account problems, please contact your provider's official customer support.
